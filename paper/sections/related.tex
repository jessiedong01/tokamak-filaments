\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Filament imaging on MAST.}
\citet{dudson2008experiments} used the midplane fast camera with automated analysis of L-mode images and BOUT simulations and found that the toroidal mode number of the edge turbulence rises with density and falls with $q_{95}$, that filament width has the opposite dependence, and that simulated filaments are born within about 2~cm of the edge in a self-generated $E \times B$ shear layer. \citet{benayed2009interelm} combined camera images with a reciprocating Langmuir probe during inter-ELM periods, showed that filaments rotate near the LCFS and move outward, with more and faster filaments at higher density, and used the radial velocities to test the sheath-connected and inertial scalings. \citet{kirk2016filaments} scanned the plasma current at fixed density and heating power in L-mode and found that the radial velocity, and to a lesser extent the radial size, decrease as the current rises, a dependence consistent with the measured divertor profiles. \citet{farley2019identification} introduced a tomographic inversion of wide-angle images onto a basis of equilibrium field lines, which turns filament identification into a problem in field-aligned coordinates, and all of these studies relied on a camera geometry model built with Calcam \citep{silburn2018calcam} for the view in use. The present work departs from them in using the 48-row strip mode at 100~kHz in place of wide-angle frames, in recovering the pixel scale from the equilibrium without a geometry model, and in processing hundreds of discharges across four campaigns where earlier studies scanned a few.

\paragraph{Blob theory and simulation.}
The sheath-connected scaling is due to \citet{krasheninnikov2001scrape} and the inertial scaling to \citet{garcia2006fluctuations}, and \citet{myra2006collisionality} introduced the two-region model used here for normalization, which \citet{dippolito2011convective} review together with its comparisons with experiment. \citet{theiler2009blob} measured the velocity--size relation on TORPEX and recovered the predicted regime transitions, and three-dimensional simulations of isolated filaments \citep{easy2014three, walkden2015characterization, paruta2018blob} find that the scalings hold in form while realistic geometry and parallel dynamics change the prefactors. \citet{zweben2007edge} review edge turbulence imaging across devices, including the relation between light emission and density fluctuations on which imaging measurements rely.

\paragraph{Methods.}
The detection and tracking steps are standard, consisting of connected-component labelling after robust thresholding, frame-to-frame assignment by the Hungarian method \citep{kuhn1955hungarian}, and Lucas--Kanade optical flow \citep{lucas1981iterative}, while slopes use the Theil--Sen estimator \citep{sen1968estimates}, confidence intervals use the bootstrap \citep{efron1993bootstrap}, and the data come from the FAIR-MAST archive \citep{jackson2024fairmast}.
