\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Public archives allow filament statistics to be measured on hundreds of discharges using an automated pipeline. MAST 100~kHz strip data was used to extract radial velocities and widths for \nTracksSel{} filaments in \nShotsStats{} discharges. A pixel scale is recovered based on the equilibrium to 10--30\% accuracy per camera configuration. Filament velocities have a median of \vMedAll~m/s, and a dependence on filament width after exposure blur correction with a log--log slope no larger than about 0.1. The filaments are located near the velocity maximum in two-region units, meaning that the velocity is nearly constant. However, the velocities are only a few percent of the velocity scale predicted by the model.

Three areas where a focused experiment or public release of a geometry model would improve upon the archives are the amplitude discrepancy, the campaign-level calibration, and the radial-only measurement geometry.
