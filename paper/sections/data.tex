\section{Data}
\label{sec:data}

\paragraph{Archive.}
FAIR-MAST \citep{jackson2024fairmast} serves the MAST data set, which covers \nArchiveShots{} discharges, as one Zarr store per discharge over HTTP, with a metadata API for discovering signals and shot parameters. Six groups are read from it, namely the midplane fast camera (\texttt{rbb}), the EFIT equilibrium reconstruction (\texttt{efm}), the summary signals for line-averaged density and Greenwald density (\texttt{esm}), the interferometer (\texttt{ane}), the core and edge Thomson scattering systems (\texttt{ayc}, \texttt{aye}), and the midplane $D_\alpha$ monitor (\texttt{xim}), while plasma current, heating scheme, injected beam power, and the flat-top interval come from the per-shot metadata table.

\paragraph{Camera.}
The \texttt{rbb} signal is the Photron SA1.1 fast camera (``bullet camera B'') at the HM10 midplane port, which views the outboard edge tangentially \citep{kirk2016filaments, farley2019identification}, and in the recording mode used here it reads out a $48 \times 256$ pixel region of its sensor at 100{,}000 frames per second with 10-bit depth, with the offset of the region on the sensor recorded with each shot. Exposure times are 1--10~$\mu$s, with 4, 7, and 10~$\mu$s the common settings, and the view string in the metadata records the lens, aperture, and filter in use and whether the camera was mounted inverted, although the SOL-side detection in Section~\ref{sec:method} makes the pipeline independent of this orientation. Appendix~\ref{app:views} lists the camera configurations that these settings define in the processed set, together with their calibration.

\paragraph{Selection.}
Of the archive, \nStripShots{} discharges have a $48 \times 256$ \texttt{rbb} recording, each a burst at the fast frame rate embedded in a slow record of the whole discharge at 1--2~kHz, and the burst lasts \burstMedianMs{}~ms in the median (range \burstMinMs--\burstMaxMs{}~ms), with a frame interval of 10~$\mu$s in \nStripTenUs{} recordings. After the time base of every recording is read, a discharge is kept if its burst overlaps the plasma flat top, extended by 20~ms on each side, by at least 2{,}000 fast frames (\nOkBurst{} discharges), if its flat top is defined and at least 50~ms long (\nOkFlatTop), and if its peak plasma current is at least \ipCandMin{}~kA (\nOkCurrent), and \nShotsCandidates{} discharges satisfy all three conditions. The burst frames inside the extended flat top were downloaded and processed for \nShotsFetched{} discharges, with a median window of \windowMedianMs{}~ms and \nFramesProcessedM{} million frames in total, and the \nShotsSlowFps{} of these with a burst frame rate of \slowFpsKhz{}~kHz are processed but kept out of the statistics so that the time resolution is uniform. \paragraph{Equilibrium and plasma parameters.}
From EFIT, reconstructed every 5~ms, the LCFS boundary contour, $q_{95}$, the plasma current, the magnetic axis position, the vacuum toroidal field at the axis, and the minor radius are taken at each time, and the outboard LCFS radius is the largest major radius of the boundary contour within 5~cm of the axis height, which is the quantity that the camera limb tracks. Line-averaged density comes from the summary signal where it exists and from the CO$_2$ interferometer summary otherwise, and the Greenwald fraction uses $n_{\rm GW} = I_p / (\pi a^2)$ with $I_p$ in MA and $a$ the EFIT minor radius. Thomson profiles exist for the later campaigns only, and a profile reaches the LCFS during the flat top for \nTeMeasured{} of the \nShotsStats{} discharges in the statistics.
